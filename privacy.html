Claude Daniel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Claude Daniel is a private tool used only by its owner, Daniel Sousa Barra, to access his own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) from his own computer. It is not offered to the public.

  • Data obtained through Google APIs is processed only on the owner's device. It is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally in the operating system's credential manager.
  • Claude Daniel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
